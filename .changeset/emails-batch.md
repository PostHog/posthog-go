Send the same email to up to 100 recipients with `Emails.Batch`, one email each, in one request, from a `TemplateEmailBatch` with `TemplateData` per recipient or a `CodeEmailBatch`. More than 100 recipients is an `invalid_recipients` error before anything is sent. The client waits at least 45 seconds for the answer. Each `BatchRecipient` has its own `IdempotencyKey`, given or made by the client, that every retry reuses. `SendBatchResult` holds a `BatchEmailResult` per recipient, in order, with `Status` `"queued"`, `"failed"` or `"unconfirmed"`; send an unconfirmed email again with the same key within 24 hours of the first request that used it, so it goes out once. A request PostHog refuses as a whole returns its `*EmailError` with a result that holds every recipient: a 413 `content_too_large` says to split the recipients into smaller batches, or to make the email smaller for one recipient, and a 400 `content_too_large` says the shared content is too large. After a lost answer, recipients stay unconfirmed even when PostHog refuses the retry, with PostHog's refusal as their error. A request whose keys an earlier request still holds leaves its recipients unconfirmed. `batch_unavailable` (409) and a `not_found` batch say to send each email with `Send`.

**Breaking for custom implementations of `Emails`.** A type that implements `Emails` itself, such as a hand-written mock, now needs a `Batch` method.
