Send the same email to up to 100 recipients with `Emails.Batch`, one email each, in one request, from a `TemplateEmailBatch` with `TemplateData` per recipient or a `CodeEmailBatch`. More than 100 recipients is an `invalid_recipients` error before anything is sent. The client waits at least 45 seconds for the answer. Each `BatchRecipient` has its own `IdempotencyKey`, given or made by the client, that every retry reuses. `SendBatchResult` holds a `BatchEmailResult` per recipient, in order, with `Status` `"queued"`, `"failed"` or `"unconfirmed"`; send an unconfirmed email again with the same key within 24 hours of the first request that used it, so it goes out once. A request PostHog refuses as a whole returns its `*EmailError` with a result that holds every recipient: a 413 `content_too_large` says to split the recipients into smaller batches, or to make the email smaller for one recipient, and a 400 `content_too_large` says the shared content is too large. After a lost answer, recipients stay unconfirmed even when PostHog refuses the retry, with PostHog's code on their error, its detail in `Why`, and a message that PostHog didn't confirm the email; the call's error says to read each recipient's result and send each unconfirmed email again with the same key. An unconfirmed recipient's message always says PostHog didn't confirm the email, so it may still go out. A 409 other than `batch_unavailable`, such as keys an earlier request still holds, leaves its recipients unconfirmed. `batch_unavailable` (409) says to send each email with `Send`. A `not_found` batch says PostHog found no template with the `TemplateID` or batch sends aren't available for the project, and to check the ID before sending each email with `Send`.

**Breaking for custom implementations of `Emails`.** A type that implements `Emails` itself, such as a hand-written mock, now needs a `Batch` method.
